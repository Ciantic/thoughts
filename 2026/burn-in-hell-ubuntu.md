# Burn in hell Ubuntu and GNOME

> [!WARNING]
> This is polemic, intentionally tribalistic Linux hit piece. This is not satire though.

Ubuntu being the most well known Linux distribution means it lives in the imaginations on those switching from Windows. Most of those switching don't want to hassle with various smaller distros so they choose the known familiar Linux distro the Ubuntu. That is where everything starts to go wrong. I did that error, although I chose GNOME version of Fedora when trying to switch away from Windows.

Problems comes from GNOME! Their choices, which probably make sense to them, doesn't make sense to those switching from Windows. It is too alien, little things aren't done well.
